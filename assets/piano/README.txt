16 unmodified MP3 samples (C2, D#2, F#2, A2 through C5, D#5, F#5, A5).
The intended practice range is C2–B5 (Korean octave labels: 0옥 도–3옥 시).
Total MP3 size: 1,220,410 bytes. The 13 outside recordings were removed.
Pitch Lab changes playback rate by up to two semitones within C2–B5,
adjusts playback gain, trims leading silence and the decoded tail after eight seconds,
and applies a short release at note-off.
Existing extended/custom pitches use the nearest boundary recording, shifted
by more semitones as needed. Their MIDI pitch is preserved; the timbre can differ.
No additional reverb or synthesized layers are mixed into the piano sound.
